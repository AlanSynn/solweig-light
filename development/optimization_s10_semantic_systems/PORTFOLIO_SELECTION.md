# Portfolio selection: optimize the product, not the candidate count

The 73 records are not 73 implementation obligations. Source audit and baseline census establish which have current coverage. Assign each one: already_shipped, new_candidate, blocked_by_proof, conditional_on_profile, rejected_previous_design, design_only, selected, rejected_now or deferred. Preserve the authored proposed record; write execution dispositions separately with source and evidence.

## Value model
For current disjoint stage service C_j and candidate improvement r_j, removable service is Delta_j=C_j*(1-1/r_j). For candidates that overlap, replace the stage with their COMBINED implementation cost; do not add Delta_j twice. Pipeline latency, throughput, peak memory, first-use and deployment complexity form a Pareto frontier, not one microbenchmark ranking.

A practical selection score is expected removable whole-entry service divided by implementation+proof+validation burden, discounted by guard coverage and uncertainty. It is a planning heuristic, not measured science. A candidate with a hard upper bound below the minimum useful effect should be rejected without more kernel work. Include preparation and cache amortization.

## Initial decision order
1. U-profile frequency and fallback wall time. If present/material, T01/T02 first.
2. Serial prepared GVF and standalone diffuse pass. They are independent and can be implemented alongside typed LW, but only when actual cost is material.
3. Resource/IO census: large hash/export/checkpoint service or conservative capacity blocking throughput may justify one system track.
4. Integrate then profile again. Tables, predicates and classification caches are assessed against the NEW fast path, not the vanished reference code.
5. One residual round. Choose at most two exact improvements or a supported accelerator region whose boundary model is plausible. No restart of the framework portfolio from zero.
6. Qualify installed default or close retained-main. Keep a final decision even when the target corpus is unavailable.

## Already-shipped baseline map
- compact binary/ternary/raw visibility and bounded exports;
- common numerical geometry recipe across standalone and simulation;
- sparse wall angle evaluation and exact compiled shadow kernels;
- guarded no-bush absorption;
- demand-specific scalar cylinder radiation;
- prepared multi-thread GVF and typed postprocess;
- persistent tile process pools, output streaming and transaction recovery;
- N9 bounded Numba AoSoA stream and A-plus decoder;
- scalar UTCI vapor setup.

Do not claim these as S10 new work. N9 native-default and previous fused/decoder failures stay frozen. Optional exact field-profile support, extra platforms and deep research are deferred unless actual production usage makes them worth the added proof burden.

## Stop rules
Stop a family after two close local variants or a decisive low-coverage/parity/performance failure. Do not change the benchmark shape to preserve a favorite candidate. A useful system-only fix can ship separately when its predeclared benefits and protections pass. After the single residual round, produce final source and merge disposition; no new open-ended list of flip conditions replaces closure.
